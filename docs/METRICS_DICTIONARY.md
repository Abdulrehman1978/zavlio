# CRM Analytics Metrics Dictionary

Metric definition version: **1**. Business timezone: **Asia/Kolkata**. Period predicates use UTC timestamps derived from business-local dates and the half-open interval **[start, end)**. Default range: 30 days. Maximum custom range: 730 days.

This dictionary is authoritative for `/crm/analytics`. “Tracked” always means the consented first-party population created under Packet 08. It is not total website traffic. One visitor identifier represents a browser storage identity, not a guaranteed unique human.

## Population and attribution rules

- Canonical person: `people.merged_into_person_id is null` and lifecycle is not `ARCHIVED`. Merged source rows never count as people.
- Successful enquiry: `form_submissions.status = 'PROCESSED'`. Analytics submission events are behavioral evidence, not business enquiries.
- Attributed person: a canonical person linked through `anonymous_visitors.linked_person_id` to a visitor in the selected tracked cohort.
- First touch: normalized `anonymous_visitors.first_source` for tracked acquisition; person reports use `people.first_touch_source`, then `lead_source`, then `unattributed`.
- Latest touch: `sessions.utm_source`, with referrer/direct categorization only for that session report.
- Self-reported source: normalized `people.lead_source`. It is displayed separately.
- Missing attribution is `unattributed`, never silently changed to Direct.
- Descriptive attribution is not evidence that a source caused a commercial outcome.

## Period metrics

| Metric                         | Business meaning and formula                                                                                                              | Source/time/filter                                                             | Denominator, nulls, and limitation                                                                |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tracked visitors               | Distinct visitor IDs with at least one selected-period session.                                                                           | `sessions.visitor_id`; `started_at`.                                           | Consented tracked population only; browser IDs are not humans.                                    |
| Tracked sessions               | Count of Packet 08 sessions started in the period.                                                                                        | `sessions`; `started_at`.                                                      | No denied/unknown-consent traffic.                                                                |
| Returning tracked visitors     | Distinct selected-period visitors having any session earlier than a qualifying selected-period session, including a prior-period session. | `sessions`; `started_at`; indexed visitor chronology.                          | Denominator, when shown as a rate, is tracked visitors.                                           |
| Tracked page views             | Count of `page_viewed` events in the period.                                                                                              | `events`; `occurred_at`; event allowlist already excludes CRM/auth/API routes. | No inference from HTTP requests.                                                                  |
| Enquiries                      | Count of successful processed form submissions.                                                                                           | `form_submissions`; `submitted_at`; status `PROCESSED`.                        | A repeat form is another enquiry, not another person.                                             |
| Start Project enquiries        | Processed submissions with `form_type = START_A_PROJECT`.                                                                                 | `form_submissions.submitted_at`.                                               | Business record, not analytics event.                                                             |
| Contact enquiries              | Processed submissions with `form_type = CONTACT`.                                                                                         | `form_submissions.submitted_at`.                                               | Business record, not analytics event.                                                             |
| New identified people          | Canonical, non-archived people created in period.                                                                                         | `people.created_at`.                                                           | Repeat enquiry does not increment this metric.                                                    |
| Attributed identified people   | New identified people linked to the selected tracked-visitor cohort.                                                                      | `people.created_at`, `anonymous_visitors.linked_person_id`, selected sessions. | Tracked-funnel numerator only.                                                                    |
| Unattributed identified people | New identified people minus attributed identified people.                                                                                 | Same as above.                                                                 | Includes consent-denied, direct CRM, and unavailable browser history; not “Direct.”               |
| People with opportunity        | Distinct canonical cohort people having at least one opportunity.                                                                         | `opportunities.person_id`; cohort determined by person creation.               | Multiple deals count once for person conversion.                                                  |
| Opportunities created          | Opportunity rows created in the period.                                                                                                   | `opportunities.created_at`.                                                    | Deals, not people.                                                                                |
| Won transitions                | Every history transition into an `is_won` stage during the period.                                                                        | `opportunity_stage_history.changed_at`, `pipeline_stages.is_won`.              | A later reopen does not erase the historical transition.                                          |
| Lost transitions               | Every history transition into a closed non-Won stage during the period.                                                                   | `opportunity_stage_history.changed_at`.                                        | A later reopen does not erase the historical transition.                                          |
| Won opportunity value          | Sum of known current `estimated_value` for opportunities entering Won in the period, grouped by currency.                                 | Won transition date; `opportunities.estimated_value/currency`.                 | Null values are excluded and counted as unknown. No currencies are combined. This is not revenue. |
| Closed win rate                | `Won transitions / (Won transitions + Lost transitions) × 100`.                                                                           | Period stage transitions.                                                      | Open deals are excluded. Zero closed outcomes returns null/“—”.                                   |
| Opportunity close duration     | Median days from `opportunities.created_at` to a closed transition in the period.                                                         | Opportunity creation and history transition timestamps.                        | A reopened deal’s historical close remains a transition observation.                              |
| Lost reasons                   | Count of Lost transitions grouped by immutable `history.metadata.lost_reason`, then reason/current fallback.                              | Lost history rows; `changed_at`.                                               | Only Lost transitions contribute; reopened losses remain historically visible.                    |
| Completed tasks in period      | Tasks whose status is Completed and `completed_at` is in range.                                                                           | `tasks.completed_at`.                                                          | Never uses `updated_at`.                                                                          |
| Declared service interests     | Distinct people attached to processed period enquiries, grouped by each declared service value.                                           | `form_submissions.payload.services`; `submitted_at`.                           | Multi-service enquiries can contribute to multiple service rows.                                  |
| Latest tracked session source  | Sessions grouped by current session attribution source.                                                                                   | `sessions.started_at`, UTM/referrer.                                           | A session metric, separate from person acquisition.                                               |
| UTM report                     | Sessions and distinct tracked visitors grouped by source/medium/campaign.                                                                 | `sessions.started_at`.                                                         | Missing UTM component is `none`; no campaign causality claim.                                     |
| Landing-page report            | Sessions and distinct tracked visitors grouped by session landing page.                                                                   | `sessions.started_at`.                                                         | Missing landing page is `unknown`.                                                                |
| Content engagement             | Consented tracked events grouped by event/content identifier.                                                                             | `events.occurred_at`; service/project/lab/insight view events.                 | Event counts are interactions, not unique humans.                                                 |

## Current snapshot metrics

These values are intentionally independent of the selected historical range and are labelled “now” or “current.” Packet 12 does not reconstruct historical pipeline, task, or daily score snapshots.

| Metric                              | Business meaning and formula                                                                                                 | Source/filter                                                            | Nulls and limitation                                                                      |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Current open opportunities          | Opportunities whose current stage is not closed.                                                                             | `opportunities.stage_id` → `pipeline_stages`.                            | Current snapshot only.                                                                    |
| Current Won opportunities           | Opportunities currently in a Won stage.                                                                                      | Current stage.                                                           | Excludes Won-then-reopened deals from current count.                                      |
| Current Lost opportunities          | Opportunities currently in a closed non-Won stage.                                                                           | Current stage.                                                           | Excludes Lost-then-reopened deals from current count.                                     |
| Current open pipeline value         | Sum of known values for current open opportunities, separately per currency.                                                 | `opportunities.estimated_value/currency`; open stage.                    | Unknown-value opportunities reported separately; no FX conversion; not projected revenue. |
| Current pipeline stage distribution | Current opportunity count per stage.                                                                                         | Current `stage_id`.                                                      | Not a historical pipeline state.                                                          |
| Stage age                           | Days since latest transition into the current stage, falling back to opportunity creation; dashboard shows median per stage. | Latest matching stage history or `opportunities.created_at`; server now. | Current snapshot.                                                                         |
| Current score distribution          | Latest `ZAVLIO_LEAD_V1` score for each canonical person, grouped by intent; missing scores are `UNSCORED`.                   | `lead_scores` latest per person/model.                                   | No interpolation and no older model substitution.                                         |
| Current score coverage              | `canonical people with current-model score / canonical people × 100`.                                                        | Canonical people and latest model score.                                 | Zero population returns null. Unscored is never zero score.                               |
| HIGH+ people                        | Current-score people whose intent is HIGH or PRIORITY.                                                                       | Latest `ZAVLIO_LEAD_V1`.                                                 | Unscored excluded.                                                                        |
| PRIORITY people                     | Current-score people with PRIORITY intent.                                                                                   | Latest `ZAVLIO_LEAD_V1`.                                                 | Score is intent evidence, not contact permission.                                         |
| Stale scores                        | Current-model scores older than 24 hours.                                                                                    | Latest score `calculated_at`.                                            | “Stale” is freshness, not quality.                                                        |
| Current primary affinities          | Canonical scored people grouped by `service_interest.primary`.                                                               | Latest `ZAVLIO_LEAD_V1`.                                                 | Different population from declared enquiries.                                             |
| Open tasks now                      | Tasks in OPEN or IN_PROGRESS.                                                                                                | Current `tasks.status`.                                                  | Snapshot.                                                                                 |
| Overdue tasks now                   | Active task with non-null `due_at < server now`.                                                                             | `tasks.status/due_at`.                                                   | Snapshot, UTC comparison.                                                                 |
| Due today                           | Active task due within the current Asia/Kolkata calendar day.                                                                | `tasks.due_at`.                                                          | Snapshot, not selected range.                                                             |
| Unassigned tasks                    | Active task with null assignee.                                                                                              | `tasks.assigned_to/status`.                                              | Snapshot.                                                                                 |

## Funnels and quality

**Tracked funnel:** selected-period tracked visitors → new canonical people linked to those visitor IDs → distinct linked people having any opportunity → distinct linked people having an opportunity with any Won transition. Downstream stages never include an untracked/non-consent person.

**CRM lead funnel:** all canonical people created in the selected period → distinct people with any opportunity → distinct people with an opportunity that has any Won transition. This includes direct and non-consent business leads.

**Lead quality by source:** period-acquired canonical people grouped by first-touch fallback source. It reports total sample size, current-model scored sample size, mean/median score excluding unscored people, HIGH+, PRIORITY, distinct people with opportunities, and Won people. No “best source” or causal winner is inferred; small N remains visible.

## Comparison, formatting, and examples

- Rolling presets compare the immediately preceding equal-length interval. Calendar-month presets compare the previous calendar month. This-month and quarter-to-date compare against the immediately preceding calendar period starting at its boundary. YTD uses the equivalent prior-year interval.
- Percent change is `(current - previous) / previous × 100`. A zero previous denominator displays “New” when current is positive and “—” when both are zero; infinity is never displayed.
- Counts use `en-IN` grouping. INR uses Indian currency grouping. Exact tables retain full values; cards may use presentation formatting only after formulas are complete.
- Example: 3 Won, 2 Lost, and 5 Open yields a closed win rate of `3 / (3 + 2) = 60%`, not 30%.
- Example: one INR 100,000 deal and one USD 1,000 deal produce two currency rows; they never produce a 101,000 combined total.

## Revenue limitation

No invoice, payment, or recognized-revenue source exists. The dashboard deliberately has no Revenue KPI. “Won opportunity value” is estimated deal value attached to a Won transition; it is not booked, collected, or recognized revenue.
